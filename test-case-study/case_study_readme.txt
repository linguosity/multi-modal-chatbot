FOLDER CONTENTS & FILE DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

This folder contains a comprehensive case study of a preschool student referral
for speech-language evaluation. The materials simulate realistic clinical
documentation that would be generated during a comprehensive speech-language
pathology evaluation in a school setting.

FILE STRUCTURE:

1. teacher_report.txt
   - Teacher questionnaire and classroom observation report completed by
     Mrs. Rebecca Chen (preschool teacher)
   - Includes academic concerns, communication observations, social interactions,
     communication breakdowns, specific classroom examples
   - Rating scales for expressive language, receptive language, articulation,
     social communication, and following directions
   - Teacher's recommendation for evaluation

2. parent_concerns.txt
   - Parent interview form completed by Maria Martinez (Sofia's mother)
   - Documents developmental history including first words, word combinations,
     speech clarity concerns
   - Family history of speech/language disorders
   - Medical and developmental milestones
   - Current concerns and goals for intervention
   - Documents Sofia's communication at home vs. school

3. standardized_test_results.txt
   - Comprehensive standardized test results and clinical interpretation
   - CELF Preschool-2 (Clinical Evaluation of Language Fundamentals)
   - GFTA-3 (Goldman-Fristoe Test of Articulation-3)
   - Detailed phonological process analysis
   - PPVT-5 (Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test)
   - EVT-3 (Expressive Vocabulary Test)
   - Summary tables with standard scores, percentiles, and clinical interpretation

4. informal_assessment.txt
   - Language sample analysis and collection details
   - Morphosyntactic analysis (grammatical forms analysis)
   - Pragmatic skills observation checklist
   - Play skills assessment
   - Type-token ratio and MLU (mean length of utterance) analysis
   - Detailed utterance transcripts with phonetic notations and grammatical analysis

5. clinical_observations.txt
   - Behavioral observations during the evaluation session
   - Attention, cooperation, and emotional response documentation
   - Frustration response patterns
   - Self-correction and learning behaviors
   - Intelligibility observations
   - Voice and fluency characteristics
   - Oral-motor and structural examination (normal findings)
   - Bilingual/code-switching observations
   - Observed strengths during evaluation

CLINICAL TERMINOLOGY & KEY CONCEPTS USED
================================================================================

This case study uses authentic clinical terminology and assessment procedures
common in speech-language pathology evaluation:

STANDARDIZED TESTS REFERENCED:
- CELF Preschool-2: Clinical assessment of language fundamentals
- GFTA-3: Articulation and phonological assessment
- PPVT-5: Receptive vocabulary assessment
- EVT-3: Expressive vocabulary assessment

LANGUAGE CONCEPTS:
- MLU (Mean Length of Utterance): Average length of child's utterances in
  morphemes or words
- Type-Token Ratio: Measure of vocabulary diversity
- Morphosyntax: Grammar including inflectional endings and grammatical forms
- Phonological processes: Patterns of sound error substitution
- Intelligibility: How well speech is understood by listeners
- Code-switching: Alternating between Spanish and English

ASSESSMENT DOMAINS:
- Receptive Language: Understanding of language
- Expressive Language: Ability to produce language
- Pragmatics: Social use of language
- Articulation: Sound production
- Phonology: Sound patterns
- Fluency: Smoothness of speech
- Voice: Vocal quality characteristics

part of the clinical record. The handwritten_notes.png is a real photograph of
SLP observation notes, demonstrating the exact input type the app must handle:
imperfect handwriting, abbreviations, sticky notes, and clinical shorthand.
